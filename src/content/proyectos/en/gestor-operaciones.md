## Description

Management app built for a small operations team: shift assignment, inventory control and report generation from a single dashboard.

## Motivation

The team used to coordinate these tasks with loose spreadsheets and chat messages, which led to shift overlaps and stock mismatches that were hard to trace. The app centralizes those three processes in one place with an auditable history.

## Current status

Actively in development, alongside its manual and automated test suite (see the linked QA case). Shift and inventory flows already have coverage; reporting and UI personalization are still being built.
